📖 How to enable ADB Network Debugging on your Android TV
  1. Enable Developer Mode: On your TV, go to Settings → System → About. Scroll down to Android TV OS build and click it 7 times until it says "You are now a developer!".
  2. Enable Network / USB Debugging: Go to Settings → System → Developer options. Toggle USB debugging and Network debugging to ON.
  3. Enter TV IP: Look at your TV's IP address in Network settings and enter it in the box above.
  4. Allow Connection: Click "Connect / Pair TV". When the prompt appears on your TV screen, check "Always allow" and press OK.
